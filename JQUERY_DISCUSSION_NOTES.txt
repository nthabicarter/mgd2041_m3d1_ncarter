jQuery practice addition for the discussion assignment

Modified files: about.html and style.css only.
Original script.js, images, and other HTML pages were not changed.

How to test:
1. Open about.html in a browser with an internet connection (jQuery is loaded from code.jquery.com).
2. Scroll to the "Want to Learn More?" section.
3. Click "Show More Info" to reveal text with a sliding animation.
4. Click "Hide More Info" to collapse the text.

How it works:
- The jQuery library is loaded with a script tag.
- $(document).ready(...) waits for the page to load.
- .click(...) responds to the button click.
- .slideToggle() slides the extra paragraph open or closed.
- aria-expanded and the button label update to reflect the current state.
